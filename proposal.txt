Project Overview:
My project takes the temperatures from three neighboring cities and presents their 
highest temperatures, lowest temperatures, and average for that specific day for the month. 
This software will be used to show trends in weather for that month in that area and 
present what the user should do in response.

Implementation:
I will use std::map to store the temperatures from an external file. The map will contain a string for the value which will represent the city.
Each city contains three lists. These lists will store the highs, lows, and average temperatures across
thirty days. 

Since each city will have 3 lists with 30 inputs, that provides 90 temperatures for each
city. The total temperatures stored will be 270. 

Simulated Events: 
The simulation will focus on the weather in three different cities in
the Bay Area which could be sunny or cold. Each time period represents one day.
The lists will be updated daily, and the simulation will generate recommendations 
for users. This includes what activities to do based on the weather.
